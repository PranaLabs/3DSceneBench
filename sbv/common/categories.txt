# The category words for naming objects (<category>__<n>). Every 3DSceneBench task gives this same list, so the list says
# nothing about any one room. make_tasks refuses a room that uses a word not listed here: add the word to its group and
# keep the groups broad. Words are separated by spaces; lines starting with # are comments.
# Each word sits under its topic, the comment line above it. Pairing by place pairs words of one topic only
# (sbv/common/scene.py topic()), so a pillow is never taken for a plant: put a new word under the topic of its kind.
# The list only grows (sbv/words.py): a released word keeps its main word and its topic and is never removed, so
# adding words moves no released room's scores. sbv/common/categories_released.txt is the released list, and
# tests/test_words.py fails when a released word changed. A word for a new kind of thing goes on its topic's line; a
# variant of a kind already here goes into that kind's group below, as an alias.
